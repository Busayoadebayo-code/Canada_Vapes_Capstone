# Canada Vapes Capstone

## About the Project

This capstone project was completed as part of the Data Analytics program at Fanshawe College in collaboration with a Riipen industry partner.

Canada Vapes operates in a highly regulated and regionally diverse market, where customer behaviour and purchasing patterns can vary across provinces. The project focused on understanding sales growth, customer retention, average order value (AOV), forecasting, and regional growth opportunities.

The final analysis aimed to answer three main questions:

* Where is growth coming from?
* Which customers and regions should Canada Vapes prioritize?
* How can average order value move closer to the $120 target?

Some original project objectives, such as deeper product-preference analysis and external market-factor analysis, could not be fully completed because the required data was not available. The final project therefore focused on the areas that could be supported reliably by the available datasets.

## Project Highlights

* Analyzed approximately **$18M in completed net sales** and **178K completed orders**.
* Found that returning customers consistently generated more completed net sales than new customers, making retention an important growth priority.
* Identified that regional AOV remained below the **$120 target**, showing an opportunity to increase basket value.
* Identified **Ontario and Quebec** as the strongest scale opportunities, while **Nova Scotia and British Columbia** were better suited for pilot testing.
* Used historical and forecast data to support future sales planning and management decisions.
* Developed recommendations focused on customer retention, basket-value growth, regional testing, and measurable campaign improvement.

## Analytical Approach

The project used two separate datasets.

The transaction-level dataset included information such as sales, orders, customer type, products, coupons, and time trends.

The customer-level regional dataset included province, customer activity, tenure, total spend, order count, and regional AOV information.

The datasets could not be reliably merged because they did not contain a validated shared customer or regional key. Each dataset was therefore analyzed separately and used to answer the questions it could support reliably.

Python was used for data cleaning, validation, KPI calculations, customer analysis, and supporting analytical work. Power BI was used to communicate the findings through executive KPIs, sales trends, customer insights, AOV analysis, and regional growth priorities.

## Key Findings

### Sales Growth

Completed net sales showed strong historical growth between 2021 and 2025. The three-year forecast also showed continued positive growth through 2028, although forecast results were treated as planning estimates rather than guaranteed outcomes.

### Customer Retention

Returning customers generated more completed net sales than new customers throughout the period analyzed. This showed that retaining existing customers and encouraging a second purchase are important opportunities for future growth.

### Average Order Value

Regional AOV remained below the $120 target. The analysis therefore identified basket-value improvement as an important opportunity.

Potential actions included:

* Product bundles
* Cross-selling
* Loyalty offers
* Minimum-spend thresholds
* Carefully targeted promotions

### Regional Growth Opportunities

Regional performance showed that high AOV alone did not always represent the strongest growth opportunity. Customer reach and addressable opportunity also mattered.

The final regional priority approach identified:

* **Scale first:** Ontario and Quebec
* **Pilot / expand:** Nova Scotia and British Columbia
* **Target selectively:** Alberta and New Brunswick
* **Monitor:** Smaller markets where strong averages may be influenced by lower customer volume

## Recommendations

The project produced four main recommendations:

1. **Align the metrics**
   Use clear and consistent definitions for AOV, regional AOV, repeat rate, and related KPI measures.

2. **Strengthen retention**
   Focus on second-purchase conversion and keeping high-value returning customers active.

3. **Increase basket value**
   Test bundles, cross-sell, loyalty offers, and minimum-spend strategies instead of relying heavily on broad discounts.

4. **Pilot before scaling**
   Test campaigns in selected regions, measure the results, and scale only strategies that show measurable improvement.
