# Canada Vapes Capstone

## About the Project

This capstone project was completed as part of the Data Analytics program at Fanshawe College in collaboration with a Riipen industry partner.

Canada Vapes operates in a highly regulated and regionally diverse market, where customer behaviour and purchasing patterns can vary across provinces. The project focused on understanding sales growth, customer retention, average order value (AOV), forecasting, and regional growth opportunities.

The final analysis aimed to answer three main questions:

* Where is growth coming from?
* Which customers and regions should Canada Vapes prioritize?
* How can average order value move closer to the $120 target?

Some original project objectives, including deeper product-preference analysis and external market-factor analysis, could not be fully completed because the required data was not available. The final project therefore focused on the areas that could be supported reliably by the available datasets.

## Project Highlights

* Analyzed a business environment representing approximately **$18M in completed net sales** and **178K completed orders**.
* Found that **returning customers consistently generated more completed net sales than new customers**, making retention an important growth priority.
* Identified that **regional AOV remained below the $120 target**, showing an opportunity to increase customer basket value.
* Identified **Ontario and Quebec as the strongest scale opportunities**, while **Nova Scotia and British Columbia** were better suited for pilot testing.
* Used historical and forecast results to support recommendations focused on **retention, basket-value growth, and regional campaign testing**.

## Analytical Approach

The project used two separate datasets.

The **transaction-level dataset** included information such as:

* Sales
* Orders
* Customer type
* Products
* Coupons
* Time trends

The **customer-level regional dataset** included:

* Province
* Customer activity
* Tenure
* Total spend
* Order count
* Regional AOV

The two datasets could not be reliably merged because they did not contain a validated shared customer or regional key. Each dataset was therefore analyzed separately based on the questions it could support.

The project team used analytical and visualization tools to clean and validate the data, calculate KPIs, compare customer behaviour, review sales trends, evaluate regional opportunities, and communicate the findings through Power BI dashboards, the final report, and the presentation.

## Key Findings

### Sales Growth

Completed net sales showed strong historical growth between 2021 and 2025. The three-year forecast also showed continued positive growth through 2028.

The forecast was treated as a planning estimate rather than a guaranteed outcome and should be compared with actual results over time.

### Customer Retention

Returning customers consistently generated more completed net sales than new customers during the period analyzed.

This suggests that retention and second-purchase conversion are important opportunities for continued growth.

### Average Order Value

Regional AOV remained below the company's **$120 target**.

The analysis therefore identified basket-value improvement as an important growth opportunity.

Potential actions included:

* Product bundles
* Cross-selling
* Loyalty offers
* Minimum-spend thresholds
* Carefully targeted promotions

### Regional Growth Opportunities

The regional analysis showed that high AOV alone does not always represent the strongest business opportunity. Customer reach and the size of the addressable opportunity also matter.

The final regional priority approach identified:

* **Scale first:** Ontario and Quebec
* **Pilot / expand:** Nova Scotia and British Columbia
* **Target selectively:** Alberta and New Brunswick
* **Monitor:** Smaller markets where strong averages may be influenced by lower customer volume

## Recommendations

The project produced four main recommendations:

### 1. Align the Metrics

Use clear and consistent definitions for AOV, regional AOV, repeat rate, target gap, and related KPIs.

### 2. Strengthen Retention

Focus on second-purchase conversion and keeping high-value returning customers active.

### 3. Increase Basket Value

Test bundles, cross-sell, loyalty offers, and minimum-spend strategies instead of relying heavily on broad discounts.

### 4. Pilot Before Scaling

Test campaigns in selected regions, measure the results, and scale only strategies that show clear improvement.

## Project Limitations

The project had several important limitations:

* The transaction and regional datasets could not be directly linked.
* Product cost, profit margin, shipping cost, payment fees, and campaign-spend data were unavailable.
* Some planned analyses could not be completed because the required variables were not included in the datasets.
* Forecast results should be updated and validated as new actual data becomes available.
* Marketing recommendations require appropriate age-verification, consent, privacy, and regulatory checks before implementation.
