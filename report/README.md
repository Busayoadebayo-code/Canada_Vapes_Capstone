# Final Report

This folder contains the final written report for the Canada Vapes capstone project.

The report presents the project background, methodology, findings, recommendations, limitations, and next steps in a professional business format.

My contribution to the report included helping interpret the findings, contributing to the recommendations, reviewing the overall business story, coordinating team input, and supporting the final written structure.

The report was completed as a team deliverable and was designed to communicate the analytical findings clearly to the Riipen industry partner and academic audience.
