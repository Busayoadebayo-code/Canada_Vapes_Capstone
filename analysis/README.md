# Analysis Contribution

The main analytical coding for this capstone project was completed collaboratively by members of the team.

As team lead, my contribution focused on coordinating the analytical work, helping the team stay aligned with the project objectives, reviewing analysis outputs, and helping connect the findings back to the business problem.

My role included:

* Coordinating team members responsible for different parts of the analysis.
* Reviewing analytical outputs and identifying areas that needed clarification or validation.
* Helping connect customer, sales, AOV, and regional findings to the project objectives.
* Supporting the interpretation of Power BI results and identifying the most important findings for the final report and presentation.
* Helping ensure that the final recommendations were supported by the analysis.
* Supporting communication between the team and the industry stakeholder when project updates or analytical questions were needed.

The major analytical code is not included in this personal portfolio because I was not the primary developer of the core analytical scripts.
